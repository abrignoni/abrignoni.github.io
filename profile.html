Professional Summary

Alexis Brignoni is a Certified Digital Forensics Examiner and CISSP with deep expertise in mobile device forensics, digital forensics and incident response (DFIR), and forensic tool development. He is best known as the creator of the LEAPPs platform — a suite of free, open-source Python tools used by forensic practitioners worldwide to parse iOS, Android, vehicle, and legal return artifacts.

His work sits at the intersection of practical forensics and community education: building tools, teaching through code, publishing research, and advocating for rigorous validation methodology in digital evidence examination.

“Tool reports are not the data. The data is the data. Validate everything that matters.”

Native Spanish speaker with active engagement in both English- and Spanish-language DFIR communities. Opinions expressed are his own and do not represent any employer.

LEAPPs Platform
🌋
LAVA — LEAPPs Artifact Viewer Application
Modern reporting and visualization tool for the LEAPPs parser family. Provides faster, efficient review of parsed forensic data with filtering and export capabilities.
📱
iLEAPP — iOS Logs Events and Plists Parser
Python framework for rapid triage of iOS and iPadOS full file system extractions and iTunes backups. Integrated into Autopsy, Cellebrite, and Paraben platforms.
🤖
ALEAPP — Android Logs Events and Protobuf Parser
Shares the iLEAPP codebase architecture, focused on Android forensic artifact parsing. Integrated into Autopsy, Atrio, Cellebrite, and Paraben platforms.
📂
RLEAPP — Returns Events Logs And Properties Parser
Parses web service legal returns and user-initiated data archives for efficient review.
🚗
VLEAPP — Vehicle Logs Events And Properties Parser
Parses extractions from vehicle infotainment systems and car data artifacts.
